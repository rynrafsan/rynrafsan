### About

I'm a final-year Computer Science & Engineering student at **Daffodil International University**, working on multimodal deep learning for low-resource South Asian languages and audio data.

- 🔬 Currently building a dual-branch multimodal system that classifies **regional Bangladeshi dialect, speaker gender, and age** from raw speech audio (ResNet-50 over Mel-spectrograms + Bi-LSTM over MFCCs)
- 📄 Co-authored a **Chakma POS tagging dataset** paper, published in *Data in Brief*
- 🏗️ Also building full-stack products — a Flutter ATM cash tracker, a CV-based pothole severity classifier, and a roommate-matching platform for Dhaka students
- 🌱 Exploring speech processing, low-resource NLP, and computer vision
